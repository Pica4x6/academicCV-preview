Halmstad University

About

I’m a Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) and Docent working at Halmstad University since 2022. Before that, I was a research visitor at Chalmers University of Technology for 2 years (2023-2025). I’ve also worked as an Associate Professor at Oslo Metropolitan University, Norway, and held a 5-year PostDoc position at Chalmers. I got my PhD in 2016 from Carlos III University of Madrid. I obtained my BSc in Computer Science in 2010 from UPM University in Madrid and a year later a MSc in Computer Science in 2011 at Carlos III University of Madrid. In 2024, I finished a MSc in NLP from UNED (National Distance Education University), Spain.

My research interests range from systems security, applied cryptography, to computational sociology. Over the last years I have worked on a number of areas in these fields, including Web Security, Biometrics or Security in RFID.

Most of my papers and ongoing work are available online; however, if you should require any copy which is not online please e-mail me, and I will do my best.
